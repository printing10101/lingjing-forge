## Publication Decision
**Decision:** Reject (No)
**Rationale:** While the paper presents novel contributions with practical significance, critical flaws in methodological rigor, experimental design completeness, and reproducibility prevent acceptance. The lack of comparative benchmarks and detailed uncertainty analysis undermines confidence in results.

## Fatal Flaws (3)
1. **Insufficient Methodological Rigor** - Parameter calibration lacks uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis
2. **Missing Comparative Benchmarks** - No comparison to existing actuator formulations or control strategies
3. **Reproducibility Gaps** - No code/data availability statements or detailed experimental protocols

## Minor Suggestions (5)
1. Add statistical significance reporting to results sections
2. Include comparative benchmarks in experimental design
3. Improve section organization for better readability
4. Provide derivation context for key equations
5. Add code/data availability statements for reproducibility

## Summary
The paper makes novel contributions to thermal actuator formulation with practical manufacturing applications. However, critical gaps in methodological rigor, experimental completeness, and reproducibility prevent publication. Revisions addressing these issues would significantly strengthen the manuscript.
